## Short description (about 150 characters)
A dark-fantasy action roguelite. Fight through the underworlds of world mythology, make choices that change the story, and decide the fate of the gods.

## Long description
The Hollowing has begun. Across the world the gods have gone silent, and the realms of the dead are bleeding into the modern city of Meridian through subway tunnels, wells and mirrors.

You are a Threshold Keeper, a transit worker who died on the first night and was dragged back by a dying guide of souls. Descend, line by line, into the afterlives of many cultures: a drowned Egyptian-inspired temple, the frozen Norse road of the dead, and the nine trials of Mictlan. At the bottom of every road waits the Hollow.

**Features**
- Fast action combat: strike, dash through danger, and swat enemy bolts out of the air.
- Roguelite loop: die, return to the Terminus station, grow stronger, and learn why you were brought back.
- Pick your path through doors that offer Gifts, Echoes or healing. Find shrines that offer dangerous bargains.
- Gifts come in Common, Rare and Epic with clear descriptions and powerful synergies.
- Meet Oru, Mara, Sable and Bryn at the Terminus. Conversations change with what you have done.
- Four Lines, four bosses, and a Reckoning at the end of each: Restore, Bind or Release the dying gods. The Ledger remembers.
- Six different endings, decided by the choices you made along the way (one of them is secret).
- Real-time lighting and shadows, smoothed pixel-art characters, and an option to switch effects off for older PCs.
- Permanent upgrades from the forge, Keeper levels, save and continue, gamepad support.

## Controls
WASD move, mouse aim, left click or J attack, Space dash, E talk, 1-5 pick cards, Esc pause, F11 fullscreen.
Gamepad: left stick move, right stick aim, X or R1 attack, A or L1 dash, Start pause, D-pad picks cards.
