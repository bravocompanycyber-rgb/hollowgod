HOLLOWGOD - Prototype 0.3 "The Terminus Update"  (Godot 3.5)

SETUP (first time)
1. Open Godot 3.5, click IMPORT, select project.godot in this folder, then "Import & Edit".
2. Press F5 to play. The game starts at the Title screen.

IF YOU USE GIT
  git pull        (get my latest changes)
  then press F5 again in Godot. If Godot asks to reload scripts, click Reload.

CONTROLS
WASD move | Mouse aim | Left click or J attack (swats bolts) | Space / Shift dash
E or Enter: talk, confirm, continue | 1-5 or click: pick a card | Esc: pause menu | F11: fullscreen

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.3
- Title screen: Continue / New Game. Your progress is saved to Godot's user folder (autosaves in the hub).
- The Terminus hub: talk to Oru, Mara Voss, Sable and Bryn. Conversations have choices and change as you progress.
- Bryn's Forge: spend Echoes on permanent upgrades. Sable's Ledger shows your record and choices.
- Five rooms per run with doors: pick the reward you want (Gift / Echoes / Mending) for the next room.
  Room 3 is an elite fight, room 4 a Shrine of Scales (bargains), room 5 the Jackal Warden.
- RPG layer: Keeper level and XP, loot rarity (Common / Rare / Epic), critical hits, bigger numbers.
- 10 Gifts, each with a clear description of exactly what it does.
- Pause menu (Esc), sound effects and ambient music generated by code, crisp text.
- Window now starts smaller (fits 1366x768 screens). Press F11 for fullscreen.

If anything goes wrong, copy the red/yellow text from the Output panel (or take a screenshot) and send it to me.
