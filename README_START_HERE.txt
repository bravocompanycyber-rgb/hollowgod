HOLLOWGOD - Prototype 0.5 "The Rime Road Update"  (Godot 3.5)

SETUP (first time)
1. Open Godot 3.5, click IMPORT, select project.godot in this folder, then "Import & Edit".
2. Press F5 to play. The game starts at the Title screen.

IF YOU USE GIT
  git pull        (get my latest changes)
  then press F5 again in Godot. If Godot asks to reload scripts, click Reload.

CONTROLS
WASD move | Mouse aim | Left click or J attack (swats bolts) | Space / Shift dash
E or Enter: talk, confirm, continue | 1-5 or click: pick a card | Esc: pause menu | F11: fullscreen

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.5
- LINE TWO: THE RIME ROAD (unlocks after you beat the Jackal Warden). Icy look with falling snow, tougher
  enemies, better rewards, its own music, its own Shrine of the Norns bargains.
- New enemy: the Charger. It telegraphs a red line, then dashes at you. Dash through it or sidestep.
- New boss: the Frost Jarl. Ground slam shockwaves (dash through the ring!), charges, bolt fans, and he
  calls reinforcements at half health. Its Reckoning (Restore / Bind / Release) has its own rewards.
- Choose your Line at the gate. The Ledger records which lines you cleared and what you chose.
- Oru reacts to new events (Line Two opening, the Jarl's fate).
- GAMEPAD SUPPORT: left stick move, right stick aim (or auto-aim), X or R1 attack, A or L1 dash,
  A talk/confirm, Start pause, D-pad left/up/right/down pick cards 1/2/3/4.
- Cards now shrink long text instead of overflowing.

FROM 0.4 AND EARLIER
