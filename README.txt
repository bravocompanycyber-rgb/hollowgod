HOLLOWGOD  (Prototype 0.7)
A dark-fantasy action roguelite by Bravocompanycyber.

HOW TO PLAY
Double-click HOLLOWGOD.exe. No installation needed.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway" (the game is not code-signed yet).

CONTROLS
WASD move | Mouse aim | Left click or J attack (also swats enemy bolts) | Space or Shift dash
E or Enter talk and confirm | 1-5 pick cards | Esc pause and options | F11 fullscreen
Gamepad: left stick move, right stick aim, X or R1 attack, A or L1 dash, Start pause, D-pad picks cards

Your progress saves automatically at the Terminus. Choose Continue on the title screen to resume.
